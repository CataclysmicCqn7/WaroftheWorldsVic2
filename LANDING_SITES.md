# Landing sites

The first cylinder always lands at Oxford (province 294), followed by two more near London on days 2 and 5 (one each from St Albans, Chelmsford, Brighton, Southampton and Salisbury).

After that, every 780 days (one Mars opposition) a wave picks **one target region** by weight, and three cylinders land at random sites inside it, on days 0, 2 and 5 (events 777200 and 777210-777230, `events/WotW_Arrival.txt`). A site the Martians already own is skipped.
